How to play

  1. Tap a hole. Its balloon grows until it touches a wall, a peg, another balloon or an empty hole.
  2. Get every star inside a balloon to clear the wall.
  3. The order you tap is the whole puzzle. In a run, one lost wall ends the run, unless you watch an ad to revive.

Questions

Can I pay to remove the ads?

Not in this version: the game is free with ads and has nothing to buy. A later update may add a way to remove the full-screen ads. The videos you choose to watch for a reward (a revive, a hint, restarting a lost wall, saving your daily streak) are always your choice. You never have to watch those.

When do full-screen ads play?

Only on the results screen, after your score. Never in your first three games of a visit, then after every second game at most, and at least 3 minutes apart. Never on a new best, and never after you press Play again.

How does the streak save work?

Your daily streak counts the days in a row you've won the daily puzzle. If you miss exactly one day, open the daily puzzle the day after and it offers to keep your streak: free once a week (Monday to Sunday), and after that by watching an ad. Then win that day's puzzle to keep it growing. Two missed days in a row can't be saved, and a saved day can't be followed straight away by another save. Streaks are never sold. Days follow your phone's date.

How do I report an ad?

If an ad seems inappropriate or wrong for children, go to Settings › Report an ad. That opens an email to us in your mail app. Tell us what the ad showed and roughly when you saw it. You see everything in the email, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Why does the game ask what year I was born?

So that younger players get ads that suit them. The game asks once, the first time you open it, before any ad. Any answer lets you play. The year stays on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone. Each time it starts, the game also asks the App Store or Google Play for your account's age range where the store gives one, and goes by the younger of the two; the store's answer is never saved or sent. If it means you may be under 16, the game never asks the tracking question below and asks Google for non-personalised ads only; under 14, it also asks Google to treat its ads as directed at a child. The privacy policy has the details.

Why does the game ask to track me on iPhone?

The first time you open the game on an iPhone, once its first screen is showing, your iPhone asks whether the game may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. It asks only if the year of birth you gave means you are 16 or older. Allow lets Google choose the game's ads using that activity. Ask App Not to Track means non-personalised ads only. The game plays the same either way, and it asks only once.

To change your answer later, open your iPhone's Settings, go to Privacy & Security › Tracking, and switch Inflate to Fill on or off. The game follows the new answer the next time it starts.

On Android there is no such question. The game asks Google for non-personalised ads only.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?

On iPhone, the tracking answer is changed in the iPhone's own Settings (see the question above). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game also shows Google's consent message, and the game's Settings has Privacy choices, where you can change that answer any time. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's settings. The full policy is under Settings › Privacy policy and at the link below.

I lost my progress, or I got a new phone.

Your best runs, your daily streak, your Adventure stars, your settings and the run you're in the middle of are saved in the app on your phone. There's no account and no cloud save of ours, so deleting the app deletes them from the phone. If your phone backs up app data to your Apple or Google account and you restore from that backup, they may come back, but we can't promise it.

If the game was closed in the middle of a run, it picks the run up on the same wall the next time you open it, exactly as you left it.
